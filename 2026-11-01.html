Learning order parameters from raw spin configurations alone

arXiv · spin models & criticality · 6/10

The abstract reports a data-driven method that learns macroscopic order parameters, including replica overlap, directly from spin configurations of Ising, stripe-order and Sherrington–Kirkpatrick models. It is a conceptual relative of autoencoder-based phase mapping. An open question is whether the method needs any knowledge of the Hamiltonian, and how it compares with a plain autoencoder.

Bayesian uncertainty floor for partially observed Ising critical systems

arXiv · magnetism + ML · 5/10

The abstract reports that at the 2D Ising critical point, the energy's irreducible error floor vanishes, while the magnetisation's stays near 0.072. It also notes that bounding that floor from a single Monte Carlo chain requires an assumption about how correlations decay. The result bears on how uncertainty is quoted for critical temperatures and exponents from finite lattices. The same paper also appeared in the theory feed.

Physics-gated hybrid predicts IN718 LPBF texture with conformal intervals

arXiv · ML for manufacturing · 5/10

The abstract reports a leave-one-defocus-out R² of 0.778 for the physics anchor model, versus about 0 for a black-box model, and 0.750 for the gated hybrid. It also reports grouped cross-validation, which holds out whole groups of data rather than random points. Texture is not a defect label, so this is mainly a validation technique worth borrowing, not a monitoring result.

ML · Magnetic Materials

Field-linear three-spin interactions go beyond pairwise exchange

arXiv · ML materials discovery · 5/10

The abstract reports that Zeeman coupling of conduction electrons generates isotropic three-spin terms, plus spin-orbit terms that couple to scalar spin chirality. The theory is validated on Hubbard trimers. Standard spin simulations assume pairwise exchange, so a natural question is whether these corrections are negligible at the fields usually simulated.

Uncertainty-driven fine-tuning of an aluminium MACE potential

arXiv · ML materials discovery · 5/10

MACE is a machine-learned interatomic potential. The abstract reports that uncertainty-driven selection of force data lowered the development force error from 22.43 to 21.52 meV/Å after four rounds, beating random sampling, while phonon drift stayed below 0.05 THz. The gain is modest, and the system is aluminium defect work rather than magnetism. It is mainly a template for active learning when data are scarce.

DFT+U+J study of anomalous Hall effect in ferrimagnetic GdCo5

Physical Review Materials · 6/10

The abstract reports a first-principles analysis of the intrinsic anomalous and spin Hall effects in the rare-earth transition-metal ferrimagnet GdCo5, using Berry curvature. It is a worked example of DFT+U applied to a rare-earth–transition-metal system. The summary does not address magnetocrystalline anisotropy, which is a natural follow-up question.

Neutron scattering finds Dzyaloshinskii–Moriya exchange behind Ni3TeO6 multiferroicity

arXiv · ML materials discovery · 6/10

The abstract reports chiral, non-reciprocal spin waves that soften in an applied field. Their condensation changes the magnetic structure and drives the electric polarization. It is experimental exchange microscopics explaining a known multiferroic, rather than a new discovery method.

Theoretical Physics

Disorder changes multicritical points differently in canonical and microcanonical ensembles

arXiv · statistical mechanics · 7/10

The abstract reports two Blume–Emery–Griffiths variants with the same free energy but different canonical and microcanonical phase boundaries. Tricritical points appear at any active-spin fraction in one ensemble but only at large fractions in the other. It is a reminder that Monte Carlo schemes can sample different ensembles when interactions are long-range.

SLAC reports experimental proof of altermagnetism

Phys.org · General physics · 6/10

Ferromagnets and antiferromagnets were long considered the only two kinds of intrinsic magnetic order. Altermagnets are a proposed third kind, and a Phys.org summary says a SLAC-led Science paper delivers conclusive experimental proof. Only the summary is available, so how the confirmation was made is unverified.

Fuzzy-sphere method gets an effective-field-theory origin

arXiv · spin models & criticality · 6/10

The fuzzy-sphere method places a 3D critical point on a sphere threaded by a magnetic monopole, making it computable. The abstract reports a framework that derives these Hamiltonians from local parent theories, including a new easy-plane ferromagnet with z=2 critical scaling. It stays far from real materials, but it makes Ising universality computable.
